Why High ROC-AUC Alone Fails at the Bedside

01 · Problem Statement
  • Cross-Hospital Prevalence Shift: Across the 4 UCI centers, CAD prevalence spans 36.2% (Budapest) to 93.5% (Zurich). Fixed thresholds collapse to 64.2% accuracy zero-shot.
  • Overconfident Miscalibration: Uncalibrated neural & tree models distort clinical decision-curve net benefit and risk stratification.
  • Silent Subgroup Disparities: Baseline models miss disease in women 3× more often than men (25.0% vs 7.4% FNR on small cohorts).
  • Correlation ≠ Actionability: Observational risk drivers (e.g., exercise angina) carry massive confounding bias (+0.371); bedside recourse must lock immutable demographics.

Architecture: PLR + UniMP Graph-Augmented Clinical Transformer

02 · Methodology
LEVEL 1 · TOKENS

PLR Periodic Embeddings

Learnable Fourier periodic features + ReLU + linear skip capture sharp AHA blood-pressure and glycemic step thresholds (Gorishniy et al., NeurIPS 2022).

LEVEL 1 · INTRA-PATIENT

[CLS] Feature Transformer

Multi-head self-attention models cross-biomarker interactions and outputs native patient-specific biomarker attention weights.

LEVEL 2 · INTER-PATIENT

UniMP Inductive Ego-GAT

O(B·k) exact cosine k-NN over 4,096 learned-space medoids with masked neighbor outcome embeddings (query self-loop strictly masked = 2 → zero leakage).

FUSION & TRIAGE

Gated 3-Paradigm Ensemble

Sigmoid gate blends tabular & cohort evidence, fused via 5-fold OOF simplex weights with XGBoost & Lossguide-HGB/XGBRF + Mondrian Conformal Triage.

  • Zero-Leakage Inductive Invariance: Perturbing eval patients produces bit-identical training logits (unit-tested in test_graph_transformer.py).
  • Standalone Neural Breakthrough: Adding PLR + UniMP lifted standalone GAT-Transformer from 0.7959 → 0.8006 on 68.6k patients — beating standalone XGBoost (0.8003) and HGB (0.8003).

Clinical Safety Guardrails: Conformal Triage, Fairness & Recourse

06 · Trustworthy AI
  • Multi-α Sex-Stratified Mondrian Conformal Triage: Clinicians tune nominal coverage live: 85% (27.4% deferral), 90% (90.22% empirical coverage, 43.1% deferral), or 95% (95.1% coverage).
  • OOD Fail-Safe Abstention: Dual max-|z-score| and learned-manifold cosine distance detectors automatically force DEFER_TO_CLINICIAN on out-of-distribution inputs.
  • Demographic Parity at Scale: On 10,289 holdout patients (6,637 F / 3,652 M), Stage-1 maintains a 0.82% sensitivity gap (Female AUC 0.7991, Male AUC 0.8037).
  • Dual Bedside Interpretability: Every prediction surfaces Level-1 biomarker attention bars + Level-2 matched historical cohort medoids with outcomes and GAT weights.
  • Immutable-Safe Counterfactual Recourse: Single-pass counterfactuals simulate BP control, lipid lowering, weight reduction, and smoking cessation while strictly locking Age, Sex, and Height (0.000 violation).
  • Causal Humility: Cross-fitted AIPW & E-value audits explicitly document residual confounding rather than overclaiming causality from observational registries.

Transparent Limitations & Path to Clinical Translation

08 · Governance & Next Steps
  • Retrospective Cohorts Only: OpenML 45547 and UCI 4-Center are retrospective registries; prospective silent-mode shadow trials are mandatory before clinical decision use.
  • MIMIC-IV Demo Sample Size: The public ICU demo has only 275 admissions (15 deaths), reported strictly as a patient-grouped pipeline demonstration rather than a model ranking.
  • Regulatory Boundary: Software is explicitly documented as a clinician-in-the-loop research prototype — not FDA-cleared or CE-marked SaMD.
  • 1. Prospective Silent-Mode Shadow Trial: Measure clinician alert fatigue, deferral turnaround time, and calibration drift under live hospital EHR streams.
  • 2. Credentialed Full MIMIC-IV & Raw 12-Lead ECG: Replace tabular stress summaries with end-to-end 1D ECG encoders fused into the Level-1 Transformer.
  • 3. Automated Shadow Trial Reporting: Join de-identified /v1/shadow/stage1|2 logs with delayed hospital outcomes via heart-pipeline shadow-report (TRIPOD+AI / DECIDE-AI compliant).
